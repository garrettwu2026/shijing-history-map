Generated offline by `scripts/v20-colors.py`, fixed lookup in `modules/polity-colors.js`.
2997 stable polity identities, 9403 union adjacency edges across all 54 snapshots.
Adjacency uses a 0.10 degree gap allowance for simplified borders. Greedy assignment
uses 24 muted mineral colors and maximin neighbor distances after 0.53 compositing
on the warm land color. Simple protan/deutan matrices and luminance are included
in the objective; these are screening aids, not a clinical color-vision guarantee.
Culture areas deliberately use lower opacity and dots. Selection alone gets gold.

The table is shared across dates and disjoint fragments. It does not claim that
all identities or all neighbors can be distinguished by hue alone. Ink separators,
labels, interaction outlines and pattern semantics remain necessary, especially
in grayscale and for tiny territories. Numerical audit is retained in the ignored
`artifacts/v20-color-report.json`; no attribution is added to map UI.
